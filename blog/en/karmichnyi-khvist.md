Sometimes life seems to go round in circles. The same kind of partner again, money “running away” again, the same fear you thought you had already dealt with. In the Destiny Matrix, such recurring scenarios are most often linked with the **karmic tail** — one of the most important and most discussed parts of the matrix.

In this article we look at what the karmic tail is, where to find it in your matrix, how to recognise that the program is active and — most importantly — how to turn it from a burden into a resource.

## What the karmic tail is

The karmic tail is the three energies at the bottom of the matrix, on the line running down from the center. In Natalia Ladini’s method they are read as **the task the soul came into this incarnation with**: unfinished lessons, habits and scenarios the person “brought with them”.

The three energies of the tail have different roles:

- **The bottom point** — past experience, the root of the program: what has already happened and what the soul knows well — sometimes too well.
- **The middle point** — how the program shows up now: the situations and states in which the tail reminds you of itself.
- **The top point (closer to the center)** — the task for this life: the quality worth developing so that the program stops repeating.

[[note: The karmic tail is not “bad karma” and not a punishment. It is a description of a lesson. A tail that has been worked through becomes one of a person’s strongest pillars.]]

## Named karmic programs

Some combinations of the three tail energies recur in many people and have their own names: for example, “Wizard and magician”, “Rebel”, “Treasure in the family” and others. A named program describes a ready-made, recognisable scenario — which makes it easier to see in your own life.

If your tail has no name, that is normal. Then read it through the three separate energies and their roles.

## Signs that the karmic tail program is active

The tail doesn’t show up all the time, but in certain situations. Here are the typical signs:

- **The same scenarios with different people.** Different partners, colleagues or friends — but the same feelings and the same ending.
- **A strong emotional reaction “out of nowhere”.** A small thing hurts disproportionately deeply — most often this is an echo of the old program.
- **A feeling of “I’ve been through this before”.** You recognise the situation before it has even unfolded.
- **A fear that has no explanation in this life.** For example, fear of being visible, fear of money or fear of trusting.
- **Repetition in certain years.** When the energy of the year coincides with the energy of the tail, the program intensifies. More on this in the article on the [program of the year in the Destiny Matrix](/en/blog/prohrama-roku-matrytsia-doli).

## How to work through the karmic tail

Working through is not a one-off action but a gradual switch of the energy from the minus to the plus. Here is a practical order.

### 1. Put the scenario into words

Reread the description of your three energies in the minus and find situations from your life that fit it. Write down 2–3 concrete examples. A scenario put into words loses half its power.

### 2. Find the point of choice

In every recurring scenario there is a moment when you act “on autopilot”: you agree, stay silent, run away, rescue. This is where the program replays itself. Noticing that moment is already half the work.

### 3. Develop the quality of the top point

The top energy of the tail is the medicine. If it holds the energy of responsibility, the lesson is to take it on rather than pass it to others. If it holds the energy of love, it is to learn to choose with your heart rather than out of fear. Look at what your top energy looks like in the plus and add one concrete action in that direction to your life.

### 4. Ask yourself the right questions

The tail reading on the site has a “Questions for yourself” section. Answer in writing — it is one of the most effective ways to see the program from the outside.

### 5. Check your decisions

When an important choice is ahead and you suspect it is “the tail again”, ask a question in the [Tarot interpreter](/?go=tarot&lang=en). It takes your Destiny Matrix into account and helps you see where the old program is at work and where your true desire is.

> A karmic tail that has been worked through doesn’t disappear — it turns into experience from which you help others.

## The karmic tail and relationships

The tail shows up especially vividly in close relationships: that is where there are the most emotions and the least control. If the same scenario keeps repeating with partners, it helps to look not only at your own matrix but also at the [couple’s compatibility matrix](/?go=pair&lang=en) — it shows which karmic tasks the couple goes through together.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can the karmic tail be “closed” for good?

The tail energies stay in your matrix for life. But once the lesson is learned, they sound in the plus: instead of recurring situations, wisdom and strength appear in the same theme.

### Is a “heavy” tail a bad thing?

No. Energies with a “heavy” reputation often give the greatest resource once worked through. Heaviness only shows the depth of the lesson.

### Where should a beginner start?

With reading the whole matrix, not just the tail: the center, talents and money will show which strengths to lean on. Start with our [step-by-step guide](/en/blog/yak-rozshyfruvaty-matrytsiu-doli).

The karmic tail explains why the same situations keep coming back and shows the way out — the quality worth developing. [Calculate your Destiny Matrix](/?lang=en), find the three energies of your tail and start small: name your scenario and notice the moment of choice.
