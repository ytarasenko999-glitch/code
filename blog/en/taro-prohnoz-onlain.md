## How to ask a question

Tarot answers more precisely when the question is open and about you:

- ✗ “Will he come back?” → ✓ “What is important for me to understand about this relationship?”
- ✗ “Will the project work?” → ✓ “What should I pay attention to so the project succeeds?”
- ✗ “What will happen in my life?” → ✓ “Which theme matters most for me in the next three months?”

[[note: Tarot is a tool for reflection, not an exact prediction. The cards help you see the situation from another side, but the decision is always yours.]]

## Which spreads to choose

- **One card** — advice for the day or a quick answer.
- **Three cards** — past, present, future, or situation, obstacle, advice.
- **Relationship spreads** — what you feel, what the other person feels, where the relationship is heading.
- **Decision spreads** — option A, option B and what will help you choose.
- **Free spread** — as many cards as you draw, without fixed positions.

## Tarot and the Destiny Matrix: why combine them

The matrix shows your constant energies — character, talents, lessons. Tarot shows how these energies play out in a specific situation right now. Together they give both depth and a practical answer.

For example: if your matrix has a strong Emperor energy in the minus (control) and the Emperor appears in a relationship spread, the reader will point out that the problem may be exactly the wish to control everything.
