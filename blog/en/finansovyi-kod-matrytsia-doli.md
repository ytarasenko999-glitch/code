“Financial code”, “money channel”, “abundance code” — this is what people often call the part of the Destiny Matrix that governs money. Here is where it is in the matrix, how to read it and what blocks the flow.

## Where money is in the matrix

In Natalia Ladini’s method the money theme is read through several positions:

- **The right point (the material)** — your resource and attitude to the material world.
- **The entry point of the money channel** — the activity through which money comes most easily.
- **What multiplies** — the energy that increases income when you live it in the plus.
- **Where love and money cross** — the balance between relationships and finances.

Together these energies make up your financial code.

## How to read the money channel

1. **Start with the entry point.** Read in which fields this energy is fulfilled — a hint at the activity through which money comes most simply.
2. **Look at what multiplies.** This energy is an “amplifier”. If it is in the minus, money comes but doesn’t stay.
3. **Check the material point.** It shows how you treat money in general: as a resource, freedom, safety or proof of your worth.
4. **Notice the crossing with love.** Financial blocks are often linked to relationships: “money or love”, “I’m valued only for what I give”.

## What blocks the financial flow

- **Channel energies in the minus.** Every energy has its own “money minus”: scattering, fear of selling, self-sacrifice, control.
- **Working outside your channel.** A person with a creative channel works for years in a rigid system — and money comes hard.
- **Family scenarios.** “There was never money in our family” — more in the article on [family programs](/en/blog/rodovi-prohramy-matrytsia-doli).
- **The karmic tail.** Some tails are directly linked to money — for example, programs about poverty, wastefulness or unfreedom.

[[note: Money in the matrix is not “how much you will earn” but “through what and how”. The strongest financial code doesn’t work if a person lives someone else’s scenario.]]

## How to open the money channel

1. Write down the fields of the energies of your channel and honestly compare them with what you do now.
2. Add one action with the “what multiplies” energy to your life — every week.
3. Reread your energies in the minus and find where you “leak” money.
4. Look at your [forecast by year](/en/forecast-by-year): in some years financial decisions come more easily.
5. For specific decisions — a launch, a job change, an investment — ask a question in the [Tarot reader](/en/tarot) with your matrix taken into account.

## The financial code and compatibility

In a couple, money is a shared theme. The [compatibility matrix](/en/compatibility) shows the couple’s money channel: how the pair earns together and where tension arises.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are there “rich” and “poor” energies?

No. Each of the 22 energies can bring money — the question is whether you live it in the plus and work within your channel.

### How fast does income change after working with the channel?

It depends on the person, but usually the feeling changes first: it becomes clear where to move and what drains your resources.

### What is the “millionaire code”?

It is the name of one of the matrix programs (5-14-19). It points to large-scale thinking and the ability to create value — but, like any program, it works only in the plus.

## Summary

Your financial code is a map of how money comes to you. Calculate your matrix, find the entry point and the energy that multiplies — and compare them with how you earn now.
