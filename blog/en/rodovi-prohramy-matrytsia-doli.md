Why do some scenarios repeat from generation to generation — in attitudes to money, in relationships, in how a family talks or stays silent about feelings? In Natalia Ladini’s Destiny Matrix the answer is **family programs**: the energies of your father’s and mother’s lines that you received together with your date of birth.

## Where family programs are in the matrix

The family square is the four corner points of the matrix, placed between the main ones:

- **Father’s line** — the points of ages **10 and 50**;
- **Mother’s line** — the points of ages **30 and 70**.

From each corner point a diagonal runs to the center with two more energies — the **program** and the **deep program** of the line. Together they show what exactly is passed down each line.

Two summary energies are calculated separately:

- **the male family line** — the sum of the points of ages 10 and 50;
- **the female family line** — the sum of the points of ages 30 and 70.

Their sum is your **social purpose**: how you fulfil yourself among people, having accepted the experience of your family.

## The resource and the task of the family line

The family line gives more than a “burden”. Every family energy has two poles:

- **in the plus** — a strength to lean on: diligence, faith, creativity, endurance, the ability to hold a family together;
- **in the minus** — repeating scenarios: poverty “because that’s how it is in our family”, divorces, silence, control, rejecting the feminine or the masculine.

[[note: A family program is not a verdict or a “curse”. It describes what you carry. A program you are aware of stops controlling you and becomes a resource.]]

## How to recognise an active family program

- You repeat your parents’ fate, although you consciously wanted something else.
- The family has “forbidden” topics: money, illness, divorce, certain relatives.
- You are drawn to professions or decisions that don’t resonate but are “the right thing”.
- Strong emotions come up in conversations about the family or at family gatherings.
- Events repeat at certain ages — often around 10, 30, 50 and 70.

## The male and female lines

**The father’s line** most often governs action, responsibility, money, power, your attitude to the outside world. **The mother’s line** governs care, closeness, the body, emotions and a sense of your own worth. A conflict between the lines shows where it is hard for you to combine “I must” and “I want”.

On CODE you can see this in the **“Path of purpose”** block under the health map: the family lines add up to social purpose, and together with personal purpose — to spiritual purpose.

## How to work through family programs

1. **Name the scenario.** Read the descriptions of your family energies in the minus and find 2–3 examples from your family’s life.
2. **Separate yours from someone else’s.** Ask yourself: “Is this my choice, or is it how things were done before me?”
3. **Take the resource.** Reread the same energies in the plus — this is the strength your family has already passed on to you.
4. **Do it differently in small things.** One conscious act outside the scenario weighs more than dozens of reflections.
5. **Remember gratitude.** Working through the family line is not a fight with it, but accepting what was and choosing what will be.

For specific decisions you can ask a question in the [Tarot reader](/en/tarot): it takes your matrix into account and helps you see where a family scenario is at work and where your true desire is.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I find my family programs without my parents’ dates of birth?

Yes. The family square is calculated from your own date of birth.

### Why are my family energies “heavy”?

Heaviness shows the depth of the lesson. Heavy energies that have been worked through often become the strongest support.

### Does the family line affect compatibility?

Yes, indirectly: the [compatibility matrix](/en/compatibility) shows the karmic program of the union — the lesson the couple goes through together, and it often echoes the family scenarios of both.

Family programs explain what you received from your father and mother — and how to turn it into a resource. Start simply: calculate your matrix, look at the energies of ages 10, 30, 50 and 70, and honestly ask yourself which of these scenarios you already see in your life.
