Every year of life has its own character. One goes by easily: things fall into place, people turn up at the right time, money finds its way. Another seems to test you: the old falls apart, plans don’t work, you want to give up on everything. In the Destiny Matrix, following Natalia Ladini’s method, this is explained by the **program of the year** — a combination of energies that “switch on” at a certain age and set the theme of that period.

The good news: you can know the program of the year in advance. Then the year stops being a lottery — you understand what it wants from you, where your strength is and where it is wise to slow down.

## What the program of the year is in the Destiny Matrix

The Destiny Matrix is built from a date of birth and consists of 22 energies — the same as the 22 Major Arcana of the Tarot. Around the main square of the matrix runs the **age circle**: from 0 to 80 years. Each point on this circle corresponds to a certain age, and each holds its own energy.

The program of the year is not one energy but **three energies sounding together**:

1. **The energy of the year** — the main theme you live through at this age.
2. **The opposite point** — the energy on the opposite side of the circle. It shows the backdrop and the challenge: how the theme of the year echoes in another area of life.
3. **The outcome of the year** — the sum of the first two. Where the year will lead if you live it consciously.

That is why two years with the same “main” energy can be lived completely differently: the backdrop and the outcome differ.

[[note: In the matrix a year is counted not from 1 January but from one birthday to the next. The “age 31” program runs from your 31st birthday to your 32nd.]]

## How the program of the year affects your life

The program of the year works like the weather: it doesn’t decide for you, but it sets the conditions. Here is how it usually shows up.

### Events and themes that “come by themselves”

If the program of the year holds energies of movement and change, moves, new projects and acquaintances appear almost effortlessly. If it holds energies of stability, life asks for order, discipline and finishing what you started. Fighting the theme of the year is pointless; it is more effective to use it.

### State and mood

The program of the year is noticeable from the inside too. A year with energies of solitude and searching for meaning is often lived more quietly: you want fewer people and more depth. A year with energies of joy and self-expression, on the contrary, pulls you towards visibility, creativity and publicity.

### Money and relationships

Every energy has its own “money” and “relationship” expression. For example, a year with the energy of responsibility and structure is good for career growth but can bring tension into relationships if the person “lives for work”. Knowing this in advance makes it easier to keep the balance.

## Plus and minus: why the same year can be different

Every energy in the matrix has two poles. **In the plus** it gives a resource: courage, intuition, money, love. **In the minus** it shows up through fears, crises, losses and recurring scenarios.

The program of the year is neither “good” nor “bad”. A hard year is usually a year in which the energy is lived in the minus: the person resists the changes the time demands, or repeats an old scenario. When you know what this energy looks like in the plus, you can consciously choose that pole.

> The program of the year is not a verdict but a hint: what to lean on and what to learn this year.

## How to live the year in the plus: 5 practical steps

1. **Find out your program.** Look at the three energies of your current age in the [forecast by year](/?go=years&lang=en) and read how they show up in the plus.
2. **Put the theme of the year into one sentence.** For example: “This year is about learning to ask for help.” A phrasing like this helps you make decisions.
3. **Look at the opposite point.** It often explains where the resistance comes from. If the theme of the year is career and the opposite point is family, the balance between them will be the main challenge.
4. **Check the year against your base.** The program of the year is laid over your personal matrix: the center, talents, karmic tail. You can read more about these positions in the article [how to read your Destiny Matrix yourself](/en/blog/yak-rozshyfruvaty-matrytsiu-doli).
5. **Ask specifically.** If there is a decision ahead — a move, a new job, a relationship — ask the cards in the [Tarot interpreter](/?go=tarot&lang=en): it takes your matrix into account and answers about your situation.

Sometimes the energy of the year coincides with the energies of your karmic tail. Such years often feel like “the same thing again”: old situations, people and fears come back. ## Frequently asked questions

### Can the program of the year be changed?

The energies themselves can’t be changed — they depend on the date of birth. But you can choose how to live them: in the plus or in the minus. That is exactly the point of knowing your program.

### Why didn’t the forecast “match”?

Most often it is because the energy is being lived in the other pole, or the year was counted from 1 January rather than from the birthday. Check which age you are looking at the program for.

### Does the program of the year work for children?

Yes, the age circle starts from zero. The programs of childhood years help parents understand what matters to the child right now, and not to push where the child is going through a natural stage.
