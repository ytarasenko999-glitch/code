The health map is one of the most interesting parts of the Destiny Matrix. It shows how energy is distributed between the seven chakras: where you are strong, where you lose strength and which areas are worth supporting. Here is how to read it.

[[note: The health map is a self-knowledge tool, not a medical diagnosis. It suggests what to pay attention to, but it doesn’t replace a doctor.]]

## How the health map is built

The map is a table of seven chakras and three columns:

- **Physical** — the energy of the Earth line: the body, actions, the material;
- **Energy** — the energy of the Sky line: inner resource, thoughts, the spiritual;
- **Emotions** — the sum of the first two: how you live it through.

At the bottom is the **energy field**: the overall total for all chakras.

## The seven chakras and what they govern

1. **Sahasrara** (crown) — connection with meaning, worldview, quality of thinking.
2. **Ajna** (forehead, eyes) — intuition, clarity of vision, goals.
3. **Vishuddha** (throat) — self-expression, communication, the right to a voice.
4. **Anahata** (heart, chest) — love, acceptance, relationships.
5. **Manipura** (solar plexus) — will, boundaries, personal power. This is the center of the matrix.
6. **Svadhisthana** (lower abdomen) — pleasure, desire, money, creative energy.
7. **Muladhara** (base of the spine, legs) — safety, the body, material support.

## How to read the map: three steps

### 1. Find repetitions

If one energy repeats in several chakras, it sets the tone for the whole body. Read its description in the plus and minus.

### 2. Compare physical and energy

A big gap between the Earth line and the Sky line in one chakra often means an inner conflict: the body wants one thing and the head another. That is exactly where tension arises.

### 3. Look at emotions

The emotions column shows how you react to strain in this area. It hints at which emotional habit takes your strength.

## How to support weak areas

On CODE each chakra has practices. A few general principles:

- **Sahasrara and Ajna** — silence, sleep, a journal, limiting information noise.
- **Vishuddha** — speaking about your needs, creativity, singing, honest conversations.
- **Anahata** — gratitude, forgiveness, breathing practices, close relationships.
- **Manipura** — decisions without postponing, sport, clear boundaries.
- **Svadhisthana** — pleasure without guilt, creativity, working with money.
- **Muladhara** — a routine, walking, a financial cushion, caring for the body.

## The health map and the program of the year

In years when the energy of the year matches the energy of a problem chakra, the theme becomes sharper. It is worth looking at your [forecast by year](/en/forecast-by-year) in advance and planning more recovery.

## Frequently asked questions

### What does the same number in all three columns mean?

The energy sounds as a whole — in the body, the resource and the emotions. It is a strong area, but in the minus it amplifies a problem “threefold” too.

### Can the health map be improved?

The numbers don’t change, but the pole does. Moving an energy from the minus to the plus feels like more strength and less tension.

### What if a particular area bothers me?

See a doctor. The map helps you see which life themes may be linked to tension, but it doesn’t make diagnoses.

## Summary

The health map shows where you draw strength and where you lose it. Calculate your matrix, choose a chakra row to read its interpretation — and start with one practice for the weakest area.
