## How the matrix is calculated: the principle

The matrix is made of **22 energies** — the same as the 22 Major Arcana of the Tarot. Every number in the calculation is reduced to the range from 1 to 22: if a sum is greater than 22, its digits are added together (for example, 27 → 2 + 7 = 9).

The base is the four points of the square:

1. **Day of birth** — the left point, personality, your “calling card”.
2. **Month** — the top point, talents.
3. **Sum of the digits of the year** — the right point, the material.
4. **Sum of the three previous** — the bottom point, the karmic tail.

The sum of all four is the **center of the matrix**, your comfort zone. From these points come the family diagonals, the money and relationship lines, the health map, purpose and the age circle from 0 to 80.

## Typical mistakes in manual calculation

- **Forgetting to reduce numbers.** There is no energy 25 or 31 — only 1–22.
- **Reducing too early.** 22 is a full energy (the Fool); it isn’t turned into 4.
- **Confusing the direction of lines.** Programs are read in a certain order, and the meaning depends on it.
- **Counting the year from 1 January.** In the forecast by year, a year runs from one birthday to the next.

Because of these small things the reading “doesn’t match”, and a person decides the method doesn’t work. That is why it’s easier to leave the arithmetic to a calculator.
