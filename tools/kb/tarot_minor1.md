# CODE deep tarot reference: Wands (Fire — action, drive, career, passion) and Cups (Water — feelings, relationships, intuition). 11 = Page, 12 = Knight, 13 = Queen, 14 = King.

## W1 Ace of Wands
Core: a spark, new initiative, inspiration, creative or sexual energy. Love: new attraction, passion igniting. Work & money: a new project, offer, idea worth starting. Feelings: excitement, desire, enthusiasm. Advice: act on the spark now. Yes/no: yes. Reversed: delays, lost motivation, a false start, blocked energy.

## W2 Two of Wands
Core: planning, a decision about direction, looking beyond the horizon. Love: thinking about the future together; choosing between staying and going. Work & money: strategy, expansion, a choice of markets or roles. Feelings: they are weighing options, thinking about the future. Advice: plan, then commit. Yes/no: rather yes, after planning. Reversed: fear of the unknown, indecision, playing safe.

## W3 Three of Wands
Core: expansion, waiting for results, long-distance prospects. Love: a relationship growing; long-distance connection; waiting for a response. Work & money: export, partnerships, first results coming. Feelings: hopeful, looking ahead with you. Advice: keep the vision broad. Yes/no: yes. Reversed: delays, plans not working, disappointment in others.

## W4 Four of Wands
Core: celebration, home, stability, a milestone. Love: engagement, moving in, family harmony. Work & money: success celebrated, a stable team. Feelings: joy, comfort, a sense of belonging. Advice: celebrate what you have built. Yes/no: yes. Reversed: tension at home, a postponed celebration, instability.

## W5 Five of Wands
Core: competition, conflict, clashing egos. Love: arguments, rivalry, playful or real friction. Work & money: competition, team disputes, struggle for position. Feelings: irritation, defensiveness, a power struggle. Advice: choose your battles, compete fairly. Yes/no: unclear, requires struggle. Reversed: avoiding conflict, inner conflict, resolution.

## W6 Six of Wands
Core: victory, recognition, public success. Love: pride in the partner, a winning moment, being chosen. Work & money: promotion, praise, success visible to others. Feelings: proud, confident, admiring. Advice: accept recognition. Yes/no: yes. Reversed: fear of failure, ego, lack of recognition, a fall from grace.

## W7 Seven of Wands
Core: defending one's position, perseverance under pressure. Love: protecting the relationship from interference; standing your ground. Work & money: competition, holding your place, defending ideas. Feelings: defensive, determined. Advice: hold your ground. Yes/no: yes, if you persist. Reversed: giving up, overwhelm, being outnumbered.

## W8 Eight of Wands
Core: speed, rapid movement, news, things in motion. Love: fast development, messages, sudden contact. Work & money: quick progress, travel, fast results. Feelings: eager, thinking of you actively, likely to reach out. Advice: move with the momentum. Yes/no: yes, soon. Timing: fast — days or weeks. Reversed: delays, haste, frustration, mixed signals.

## W9 Nine of Wands
Core: resilience, last stand, caution after hardship. Love: guarded heart after past pain; testing trust. Work & money: tired but close to the finish. Feelings: wary, protective, afraid of being hurt again. Advice: one last push, but rest. Yes/no: rather yes, with persistence. Reversed: exhaustion, paranoia, giving up.

## W10 Ten of Wands
Core: burden, overload, responsibility carried alone. Love: one partner carrying everything; a heavy relationship. Work & money: overwork, too many tasks, success that costs too much. Feelings: overwhelmed, stressed, burdened. Advice: delegate and drop what isn't yours. Yes/no: possible but heavy. Reversed: release of burden, or collapse under it.

## W11 Page of Wands
Core: enthusiasm, exploration, a message about a new venture. Love: flirtation, a young or playful energy. Work & money: new idea, learning, a creative offer. Feelings: curious, excited, playful interest. Advice: explore with curiosity. Yes/no: yes. Reversed: immaturity, scattered ideas, bad news, procrastination.

## W12 Knight of Wands
Core: passion, adventure, impulsive action, a bold move. Love: a passionate but possibly unstable admirer; a whirlwind romance. Work & money: bold moves, travel, change of job. Feelings: hot attraction, impatience. Advice: act boldly, but finish what you start. Yes/no: yes, quickly. Reversed: recklessness, anger, commitment issues, delays.

## W13 Queen of Wands
Core: confidence, warmth, charisma, independence, creative leadership. Love: an attractive, self-assured person; passionate loyalty. Work & money: leadership, creative business, visibility. Feelings: confident attraction, admiration. Advice: be visible and warm. Yes/no: yes. Reversed: jealousy, insecurity, domineering, burnout.

## W14 King of Wands
Core: visionary leadership, entrepreneurship, bold decisions. Love: a strong, passionate, leading partner. Work & money: business owner energy, inspiring others. Feelings: decisive, wants to lead and win. Advice: lead with vision. Yes/no: yes. Reversed: tyranny, impulsiveness, arrogance, unrealistic expectations.
