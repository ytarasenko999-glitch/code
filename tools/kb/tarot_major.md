# CODE deep tarot reference: Major Arcana (RWS numbering; M8 = Strength, M11 = Justice). Fields: Core; Love; Work & money; As another person's feelings/thoughts; Advice; Yes/no lean; Reversed nuance.

## M0 The Fool
Core: a leap into the unknown, a fresh cycle, trust in life, freedom from old rules; the beginning before experience.
Love: new, light, playful connection; falling in love without guarantees; a relationship that needs freedom. In an existing couple — a wish to refresh, travel, play.
Work & money: a new job, start-up, career change, freelance; money spent impulsively; opportunity for those willing to try.
Feelings/thoughts: light-hearted interest, curiosity, excitement, but little thought about the future; they enjoy the moment and avoid heavy talks.
Advice: take the first step, but look where you put your foot.
Yes/no: rather yes (for new things), unclear for stability.
Reversed: recklessness, naivety, fear of the leap, chaos, a plan without ground; or someone not ready for commitment.

## M1 The Magician
Core: will becoming action, skill, all tools at hand, the power of the word, a strong start.
Love: active courting, charm, the initiative of one side; strong attraction through conversation and wit.
Work & money: launching a project, negotiations, sales, presentations; talent turning into income; a good moment to start.
Feelings/thoughts: purposeful interest, they are thinking about how to act and win; confident, focused, sometimes calculating.
Advice: use what you already have; speak and act now.
Yes/no: yes.
Reversed: manipulation, empty words, trickery, untapped talent, scattered energy, a person who promises more than gives.

## M2 The High Priestess
Core: intuition, hidden knowledge, the subconscious, waiting, secrets, inner wisdom.
Love: a deep but unspoken connection; mystery; feelings not yet shown; a secret relationship or unacknowledged attraction.
Work & money: information not yet available; study, research, behind-the-scenes work; do not sign before you know everything.
Feelings/thoughts: they feel more than they say; hidden interest, guardedness, observing and analysing; deep but silent emotions.
Advice: listen to intuition, wait for clarity, don't force.
Yes/no: unclear — the answer is still hidden.
Reversed: secrets surfacing, ignored intuition, coldness, gossip, information withheld, a hidden rival.

## M3 The Empress
Core: abundance, fertility, beauty, nurturing, creativity, pleasure, growth.
Love: warmth, tenderness, sensuality, care; a relationship that grows; family, possibly children or a home.
Work & money: growth, profit, creative projects, beauty, comfort; things blossom with time and care.
Feelings/thoughts: tender, caring, warm feelings; a wish to take care and be close; physical attraction.
Advice: nourish yourself and what you create; allow pleasure.
Yes/no: yes.
Reversed: smothering care, dependence, creative block, overspending, neglect of self, jealousy.

## M4 The Emperor
Core: structure, authority, stability, protection, leadership, the father figure.
Love: a reliable, serious partner; stability; a relationship with clear roles; can feel controlling.
Work & money: career growth, management, business, contracts, stability, power and responsibility.
Feelings/thoughts: serious intentions, a wish to protect and control; thinking in practical terms, «how to build this».
Advice: create order; take responsibility; set boundaries.
Yes/no: yes, with effort and structure.
Reversed: domination, rigidity, control, abuse of power, weakness or absent authority, chaos.

## M5 The Hierophant
Core: tradition, institutions, teaching, mentors, shared values, rituals, marriage.
Love: a serious, traditional union; marriage; shared values; family approval matters.
Work & money: big organisations, education, rules, official procedures; a mentor helps.
Feelings/thoughts: they think in terms of «what is right», duty, commitment; respect; may be held back by norms or family opinion.
Advice: seek wise counsel; act by your values.
Yes/no: yes, if done properly.
Reversed: rebellion, dogma, hypocrisy, broken rules, unconventional union, pressure of others' expectations.

## M6 The Lovers
Core: love, union, alignment of values, an important choice of the heart.
Love: strong mutual attraction, harmony, a soul connection; or a choice between two people.
Work & money: partnership, a choice of path, a deal based on shared values.
Feelings/thoughts: strong romantic feelings, attraction, longing to be together; they feel a deep connection.
Advice: choose with the heart and take responsibility for the choice.
Yes/no: yes (in love), choice-dependent otherwise.
Reversed: disharmony, a love triangle, indecision, misaligned values, temptation, a breakup over values.

## M7 The Chariot
Core: willpower, victory, movement, travel, determination, control of opposites.
Love: active pursuit, quick development, a relationship in motion; or prioritising goals over feelings.
Work & money: progress, a breakthrough, a promotion, competition won, a trip or move.
Feelings/thoughts: determined, they want to win you or move forward; focused on action; sometimes emotionally guarded.
Advice: steer clearly toward one goal.
Yes/no: yes, through effort and speed.
Reversed: lack of direction, aggression, loss of control, obstacles, delays, running in circles.

## M8 Strength
Core: courage, inner strength, compassion, patience, gentle control of instincts.
Love: passion held with tenderness; patience with a partner; a strong bond surviving tests.
Work & money: endurance, overcoming difficulties, leading softly; you can handle this.
Feelings/thoughts: strong, passionate feelings they try to keep in check; protective, patient, attracted.
Advice: gentle strength wins; stay calm and confident.
Yes/no: yes.
Reversed: self-doubt, low energy, raw emotions, aggression, force instead of gentleness, burnout.

## M9 The Hermit
Core: solitude, reflection, inner search, wisdom, a pause from the world.
Love: distance, need for space; a period of being alone; a partner who withdraws to think.
Work & money: research, expertise, working alone; a pause before a decision; slow results.
Feelings/thoughts: they are withdrawn, thinking deeply, possibly about you, but not ready to talk; analysing, lonely.
Advice: take time to reflect; seek your own answer.
Yes/no: rather no (not now) / wait.
Reversed: isolation, loneliness, refusal of help, or a return from solitude; stubbornly closed.

## M10 Wheel of Fortune
Core: change, cycles, luck, turning points, fate in motion.
Love: a fateful meeting, a turn in the relationship, a return of someone from the past.
Work & money: sudden opportunities, ups and downs, a lucky break; things change fast.
Feelings/thoughts: their feelings are changing; they sense something important is happening; unpredictable.
Advice: catch the moment, stay flexible.
Yes/no: rather yes; the situation is turning.
Reversed: bad luck, resistance to change, repeating cycles, delays, losing control of events.

## M11 Justice
Core: truth, fairness, cause and effect, decisions, contracts, law.
Love: an honest, balanced relationship; a decision about the future; consequences of past actions.
Work & money: contracts, documents, legal matters, fair pay; the outcome you deserve.
Feelings/thoughts: they are weighing things up rationally; trying to be fair; thinking about rights and wrongs.
Advice: be honest; decide rationally; follow through.
Yes/no: yes if you acted fairly; balanced.
Reversed: injustice, dishonesty, a legal problem, avoiding responsibility, bias, a delayed decision.

## M12 The Hanged Man
Core: pause, suspension, surrender, a new perspective, sacrifice.
Love: a relationship in limbo; waiting; sacrifice for someone; seeing the partner differently.
Work & money: delays, a stalled project, waiting for payment; a time to rethink.
Feelings/thoughts: they feel stuck, uncertain, unable to act; may feel they are sacrificing.
Advice: stop pushing; look from another angle.
Yes/no: not now / wait.
Reversed: stalling, martyrdom, indecision, endless waiting; or the end of a pause and a release.

## M13 Death
Core: an ending that clears the way, transformation, the close of a chapter.
Love: the end of a stage or of a relationship; deep transformation of the bond; letting go.
Work & money: leaving a job, closing a project, restructuring; a new stage begins after.
Feelings/thoughts: they feel something must end or change; heavy, transformational emotions; closing a chapter.
Advice: let go of what is over.
Yes/no: no for the old, yes for the new.
Reversed: resisting change, clinging, stagnation, a slow painful ending, fear of the new.

## M14 Temperance
Core: balance, moderation, healing, patience, harmony, blending.
Love: calm, harmonious connection; reconciliation; healing after conflict; patience.
Work & money: steady progress, cooperation, balancing several things; moderate gains.
Feelings/thoughts: calm, warm, balanced feelings; they want harmony and peace with you.
Advice: find the middle way; be patient.
Yes/no: rather yes, gradually.
Reversed: imbalance, excess, impatience, conflicts, lack of harmony.

## M15 The Devil
Core: attachment, temptation, passion, material power, fear, dependence.
Love: strong sexual attraction; obsession; a dependent or toxic bond; jealousy and control.
Work & money: money and power, ambition; also debts, unethical schemes, a job one cannot leave.
Feelings/thoughts: intense desire, obsession, possessiveness; they can't stop thinking of you, but it is not always healthy.
Advice: see what binds you and choose freedom.
Yes/no: yes, but at a price.
Reversed: release from dependence, breaking chains; or deepening addiction recognised.

## M16 The Tower
Core: sudden upheaval, collapse of false structures, revelation, shock.
Love: a sudden breakup, a big argument, a shocking truth revealed; or a breakthrough of feelings.
Work & money: job loss, collapse of a project, sudden expenses; freedom after the fall.
Feelings/thoughts: shaken, confused, agitated; something broke for them; strong emotions.
Advice: let what is false fall; build on truth.
Yes/no: no.
Reversed: avoiding inevitable change, a crisis postponed, or fear of disaster.

## M17 The Star
Core: hope, inspiration, healing, faith, renewal after hardship, a guiding light.
Love: a hopeful, soulful connection; healing after pain; idealised love.
Work & money: inspiration, creative success, recognition, a good outlook.
Feelings/thoughts: hope, tender admiration, they see you as special; inspired and optimistic.
Advice: trust and keep going; show your light.
Yes/no: yes.
Reversed: disappointment, lack of faith, insecurity, dreams without action.

## M18 The Moon
Core: illusion, intuition, fear, uncertainty, the subconscious, hidden things.
Love: confusion, unclear feelings, jealousy, secrets, idealisation or deception.
Work & money: unclear situations, hidden information, risk of deception; check documents.
Feelings/thoughts: confused, anxious, mixed feelings; they themselves don't understand what they feel; fantasies.
Advice: don't trust appearances; listen to intuition and check facts.
Yes/no: unclear.
Reversed: truth coming out, fear fading, clarity returning; or a deeper self-deception.

## M19 The Sun
Core: joy, success, clarity, vitality, warmth, childhood happiness.
Love: happiness, openness, a warm and bright relationship; possibly children.
Work & money: success, recognition, prosperity, a project that shines.
Feelings/thoughts: genuine joy, warmth, openness; they feel good with you and think of you with a smile.
Advice: be open and joyful; act confidently.
Yes/no: yes.
Reversed: temporary sadness, delayed success, excessive optimism or ego.

## M20 Judgement
Core: awakening, a call, renewal, reckoning, rebirth, a decision.
Love: return of the past, a second chance, a decisive step, forgiveness.
Work & money: a calling, a new career level, results of past efforts, an important decision.
Feelings/thoughts: they are reassessing, recalling the past, maybe regret or a wish to try again.
Advice: answer the call; forgive and move on.
Yes/no: yes.
Reversed: self-doubt, refusal to change, guilt, missed chance, harsh judgement.

## M21 The World
Core: completion, wholeness, achievement, integration, travel, a new level.
Love: a harmonious, complete union; a happy result; a relationship reaching maturity.
Work & money: success, completion of a big project, international prospects.
Feelings/thoughts: fulfilment, completeness, they feel whole and at peace with you.
Advice: complete what you started and celebrate.
Yes/no: yes.
Reversed: incomplete business, delays, lack of closure, fear of the next step.
