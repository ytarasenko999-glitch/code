# CODE deep tarot reference: Swords (Air — thoughts, words, decisions, conflict) and Pentacles (Earth — money, work, body, home). 11 = Page, 12 = Knight, 13 = Queen, 14 = King.

## S1 Ace of Swords
Core: clarity, truth, breakthrough, a decisive idea. Love: honest conversation, clarity in feelings. Work & money: a clear decision, new idea, winning argument. Feelings: they see things clearly, rational interest. Advice: speak the truth. Yes/no: yes. Reversed: confusion, misjudgement, harsh words.
